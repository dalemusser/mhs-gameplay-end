# v0.2.0 — the smaller bundle

*2026-09-22. Status: LIVE at `https://cdn.adroit.games/mhs/end/v0.2.0/`, selected on the Dev MHS collection; Dale confirmed it plays and signed off on the visuals. Shipped player archived as `ceremony_v9.html` + `lib/player_v9.js`. Review shots (`dist/v0.2.0-review-shots/`: orig-*.png vs opt-*.png
at Toppo's opening line, a Tera line and the celebration).*

The ceremony streams at the end of the game, so its size is the wait a
student sees before Begin enables. v0.1.9 shipped 71 MB. The pipeline in
`tools/optimize.sh` (repeatable from the originals, tagged `assets-original`
in git) brings the shipped tree to about 38 MB, of which 9.4 MB is the
vendored Babylon runtime that CloudFront serves brotli-compressed.

| Asset | Before | After | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Characters (5 GLB) | 44.1 MB | 17.2 MB | dropped the 10 clips the player never references (Angry, Defeated, Dismissing, Intro, Jog, Run, TurnL, TurnR, WalkStop, ShakeHeadNo); removed 814 orphan samplers per rig (translation samplers left behind when the walk cycle was corrected — a third of every clip's bytes); resampled the 15 remaining clips at tolerance 1e-3; dedup + prune; textures → WebP capped at 1024 px (`EXT_texture_webp`; the normal maps were 2048 px PNGs of 2.2–2.7 MB) |
| Voice (27 mp3) | 4.5 MB | 2.3 MB | 64 kbps mono (was 128); durations unchanged, so the word timings in `ceremony_audio.json` still line up |
| Music (5 mp3) | 6.6 MB | 4.4 MB | 128 kbps (was 192) |
| Holo images (33 jpg) | 5.5 MB | 2.7 MB | re-encoded q4, at most 1280 px wide |
| Logo | 629 KB | 111 KB | WebP q90, 1024 px (`assets/logo/mhs-logo.webp`; the player's `EMBLEM_LOGO` points at it) |
| SFX (3 mp3) | 275 KB | 208 KB | 96 kbps |

Kept on purpose: all morph targets and bone names (the facial layers key on
them), the eyeball textures, `GestureUp` (the delivery clip for thoughtful,
serious and neutral lines), `Surprised`, and the three mingle clips.

Not done, with reasons: mesh compression (Draco or meshopt need a decoder
Babylon fetches from its CDN by default, against the no-public-CDN rule; the
geometry is only ~300 KB per rig anyway); sharing one animation library
across the five rigs (another ~13 MB, but it needs runtime retargeting and
the rigs' bone names differ in places — a later release if the wait still
matters); a tree-shaken Babylon build (needs a bundler).

Player v9: `GLB_V` 17, `HOLO_V` 3, `AUDIO_V` 2, logo `.webp`; `EMBED_V` 3.
Nothing in the embed contract changes.

**Verification.** Resolver tests green; the repo tree plays through Begin into

screenshots match the originals. The real check remains the host page on Dev
